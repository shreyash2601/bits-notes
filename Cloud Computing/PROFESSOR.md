EC2 (Mid-Semester) Exam Pattern & Instructions
To help you prepare effectively for the upcoming EC2 (Mid-Semester) examination, the following details have been provided.
Question Paper Pattern
The paper consists of exactly 3 questions, each carrying 10 marks for a total of 30 marks.
Each main question is broken down into sub-questions (e.g., a, b, c).
You must answer all the sub-questions consecutively, and every answer must clearly state the relevant sub-question number.
Question Style & Preparation Strategy
Application & Analysis (2 Questions): Two of the three questions are heavily scenario-based. You will be given specific business constraints/architecture scenarios etc. You are expected to think critically, apply the concepts, and justify your choices or troubleshoot issues.
Direct Concepts (1 Question): The third question will be more straightforward, testing your understanding of core cloud terminology and concepts.
Mathematical Component & Calculators
You will encounter sub-questions involving basic math. 
While the arithmetic is simple, you can plan to use a basic calculator for answering.
